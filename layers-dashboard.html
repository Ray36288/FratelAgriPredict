DECISION SUPPORT

Forecast & bird value

Forecast a custom horizon in days, weeks or years. Compare feed needs, cash expenditure and the cost of keeping a bird to a selected age.

Build a prediction

The project horizon defaults to two years. Override the expected harvest/end date for each batch where required.

Forecast basis: Record actual daily feed intake in Feed & Inventory after producing feed. The system averages recorded feeding days per live bird, then calculates the selected day, week, month or year requirement. Stage reference targets do not replace actual intake. If no intake has been recorded, the forecast will report that more records are needed.
Choose a batch and prediction period to calculate a forecast.

What could one bird cost at any age?

Historical cost uses recorded costs up to the selected age. For future ages, the forecast extends current cost using recent recorded daily feed intake and recurring expense patterns.

Select an age to calculate the bird's estimated production cost.

Price-change simulation

Change the ingredient price and see its effect on feed cost and future spending. This changes a scenario only; it does not alter saved purchase prices.
